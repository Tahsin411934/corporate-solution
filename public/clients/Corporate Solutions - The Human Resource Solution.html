Payroll Administration

Corporate Solutions is one of the industry-leading HR outsourcing companies that can handle all of your payroll needs. We handle all payroll tax deposits, 941, W-2, W-4, and I-9 processing.

DETAILED PAYROLL REPORTING

We provide you easy-to-use tools to calculate your payroll hours and we do the rest. All payroll reports, billing, registers, and department summaries can be provided.

DIRECT DEPOSIT / CERTIFIED PAYROLL DELIVERY

Corporate Solutions offers a safe and convenient direct deposit option for your employees and we offer timely and certified payroll delivery.

PAYROLL DEDUCTIONS

Corporate Solutions can also process any payroll deductions, such as for uniforms, tools, child support, or court ordered garnishments

Workers’ Compensation

Corporate Solutions can reduce your administrative tasks and stabilize your workers’ compensation costs.

NO DOWN PAYMENT

With no down payment for workers’ compensation, you will improve your cash flow since premium is based on actual payroll. No surprising expenses at the end of the year.

STABILIZING WORKERS’ COMP COSTS

By spreading your workers’ compensation risks over a pool of thousands of employees, Corporate Solutions can stabilize your costs.

ESTABLISHED “BACK-TO-WORK” PROGRAM

Enjoy added protection to your business with Corporate Solutions™ compensation coverage.

CERTIFICATES OF INSURANCE

When needed, Corporate Solutions can provide you with up-to-date documentation and proof of workers’ compensation insurance.

PROACTIVE RISK CONSULTING

Corporate Solutions has risk consultants that proactively identify potential hazards and can recommend safety solution programs such as, comprehensive safety policies, customized safety manuals, OSHA regulation knowledge, and accident investigation services
